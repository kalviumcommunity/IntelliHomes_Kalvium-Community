Community meeting notes: the board approved the annual maintenance fee of 1500 per quarter, payable to the HOA account.

Swimming pool timings are 6 AM to 10 PM, and residents must show their access card at the gate.

Clubhouse bookings for private events are handled through the community portal and require 48 hours notice.

Parking rules: each unit gets one reserved space, and guest parking is available near the main entrance.
