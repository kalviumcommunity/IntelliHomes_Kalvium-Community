Here are three common property documents that you may need to verify:

1. **Title Deed**: Also known as the deed of conveyance, this document proves ownership of the property and includes details such as the seller's name, buyer's name, purchase price, and any encumbrances (liens or mortgages).
2. **Property Survey Report**: This document provides a detailed description of the property's boundaries, including measurements and any easements or restrictions that may affect the property.
3. **Mortgage Deed** (if applicable): If there is an outstanding mortgage on the property, this document will show the lender's name, loan amount, interest rate, and repayment terms.

Please note that these documents may vary depending on your location and specific circumstances. It's always a good idea to consult with a real estate expert or attorney for guidance on verifying property documents in your area.
